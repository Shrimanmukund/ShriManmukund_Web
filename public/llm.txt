# Shri Manmukund Hospital, Amravati

> Specialized Proctology, General & Laparoscopic Surgery, and Integrated Ayurvedic Care in Amravati, Vidarbha, Maharashtra, India. Established in 2011. Over 16,000 surgical procedures performed.

Shri Manmukund Hospital is a premier anorectal and surgical hospital located in Amravati, Maharashtra, India. The institution combines modern minimally invasive surgical modalities (laser proctology, keyhole laparoscopy) with classical Ayurvedic parasurgical treatments (Ksharsutra therapy, Uttarbasti, Panchakarma). Founded and directed by Dr. Vipin Tongale (M.S. General Surgery, FIAGES) and Dr. Swati Tongale (M.D. Ayurveda), the hospital provides advanced, empathetic, and evidence-grounded treatment for patients across Vidarbha, Western Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and neighboring regions.

## Anorectal & Proctology Specialties
- [Anorectal Care Overview](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/anorectal-care/): Diagnosis, conservative management, and surgical cures for piles, fissures, fistulas, and abscesses.
- [Piles (Hemorrhoids) Treatment](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/anorectal-care/piles/): Grading, medical therapy, Laser Hemorrhoidoplasty (LHP), and surgical excision.
- [Anal Fissure Treatment](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/anorectal-care/anal-fissure/): Conservative care, lateral internal sphincterotomy (LIS), and Ayurvedic healing balms.
- [Anal Fistula Treatment](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/anorectal-care/anal-fistula/): Low, high, and complex horseshoe fistulas managed via Ksharsutra and FiLaC laser with preserved sphincter continence.
- [Pilonidal Sinus Treatment](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/pilonidal-sinus/): Minimally invasive pit picking, laser ablation (SiLaC), and Ksharsutra ligation.
- [Laser Proctology](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/laser-proctology/): Painless, sutureless 1470nm diode laser procedures with day-care discharge.
- [Ksharsutra Therapy](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/ksharsutra/): World-renowned Ayurvedic parasurgical technique utilizing medicated alkaline thread with negligible recurrence rates.

## General Surgery & Laparoscopy
- [General & Laparoscopic Surgery](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/general-surgery/): Keyhole laparoscopic and open surgical interventions for abdominal pathologies.
- [Hernia Repair](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/general-surgery/hernia/): Inguinal, umbilical, and incisional hernia repair with tension-free prosthetic mesh.
- [Gallbladder Surgery](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/general-surgery/gallbladder/): Laparoscopic cholecystectomy for symptomatic cholelithiasis (gallstones).
- [Appendectomy](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/general-surgery/appendix/): Emergency and elective laparoscopic removal of inflamed appendix.

## Ayurvedic & Female Care Unit
- [Ayurveda & Panchakarma](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/ayurveda/): Classical detoxification, Vaman, Virechan, Basti, and chronic digestive disorder management.
- [Female Proctology & Care](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/female-care/): Dedicated female consultation wing led by Dr. Swati Tongale ensuring complete privacy.
- [Infertility & Uttarbasti](https://shrimanmukundhospital.com/services/female-care/infertility/): Intra-uterine medicinal instillation for tubal blockage and uterine health.
